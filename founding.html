How the Mystery Box works

  1. Step 1

    Sign up and become one of the first 100 at your school.

  2. Step 2

    Stay active on TALK for 14 days. (You don't need to be perfect. Just show up most days.)

  3. Step 3

    Refer friends. The more people you bring, the bigger your box gets.

  4. Step 4

    After 14 days, tap "Claim your box" in the app and our team will tell you when and where to pick it up on campus.

Boxes can include stickers, wristbands, journals, T-shirts, water bottles, keychains, plaques and more. Every box has something in it.
